⚖️ ConsentFair

An autonomous, locally-run browser extension that audits website cookie banners against GDPR and the India DPDP Act 2023.

Get it for Microsoft Edge

📊 Autonomous Scoring

Instantly evaluates cookie consent banners and generates a 100-point compliance score based on strict regulatory heuristics.

🕵️ Detect Dark Patterns

Identifies visual asymmetry, negative language framing, and calculates the click-path friction required to reject tracking.

📄 Forensic Audit Reports

Generates professional offline HTML reports complete with a localized viewport screenshot of the exact banner state for your records.

🔒 100% Local & Private

Built for academic research. Zero external servers, zero data tracking, and zero APIs. Everything happens securely in your browser.

Developed for academic research by Nikita Tapali at Amity University.
