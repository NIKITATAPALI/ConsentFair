HOW IT WORKS

ConsentFair runs a fully autonomous forensic audit the moment you land on any website. No clicking required. No data leaves your device.

STEP 01 🧹
Forensic Wipe

In bulk mode, all domain cookies and cache are cleared first to ensure a pristine, first-visit environment for accurate scanning.

browsingData API
STEP 02 🌐
DOM Scan

The content script searches the entire DOM — including nested iframes — using 60+ CSS selectors and banner keyword matches across 7 languages.

content.js
STEP 03 ↕️
Scroll & Wait

The page is scrolled to the bottom and back to trigger lazy-loaded and scroll-dependent banners. Up to 15 retry cycles are attempted.

Auto-retry
STEP 04 🔬
Pattern Analysis

All 10 dark pattern detectors fire: button styles, pre-ticked checkboxes, confusing language, hidden info, nagging visits, visual hierarchy and more.

rules.js
STEP 05 📊
Score & Report

A 100-point compliance score, Consent Asymmetry Index (CAI), cookie inventory, and a downloadable forensic HTML report are generated instantly.

background.js

ALL 10 DARK
PATTERNS

ConsentFair is the only free tool that actively detects all 10 categories defined by GDPR regulators. Each pattern carries a weighted point deduction.

SEVERITY SCALE
Critical — −20 to −25 pts
High — −15 pts
Medium — −10 pts
SCORE TIERS
✅ 70–100  Compliant ⚠️ 40–69  Partially Compliant ❌ 0–39   Non-Compliant
DP-FA
🚫
Forced Action

No explicit reject button provided. Users can only accept, with no way to decline without hunting for alternatives.

−25 pts · Critical
DP-PT
☑️
Pre-ticked Boxes

Non-essential cookie categories arrive pre-selected. Valid consent must be an active opt-in, never a passive default.

−20 pts · Critical
DP-RM
🪤
Roach Motel

Accepting is one click; rejecting requires navigating into a settings panel. Effort asymmetry by design.

−15 pts · High
DP-PB
📦
Purpose Bundling

All cookie purposes lumped into one binary choice. No granular control over analytics vs. marketing vs. essential.

−15 pts · High
DP-FH
👁️
False Hierarchy

Accept button is bold, coloured, prominent. Reject button is grey, small, or outlined — steering users visually.

−15 pts · High
DP-VM
🎨
Visual Misdirection

Unequal backgrounds, sizes, or font weights between Accept and Reject buttons that nudge users toward consent.

−10 pts · Medium
DP-UF
😨
Urgency / Fear

Language like "less secure", "limited features", or "not recommended" attached to the reject path to create anxiety.

−10 pts · Medium
DP-HI
🔍
Hidden Information

Key details buried in tiny text (<10px), collapsed accordions, or a wall of text over 1200 characters long.

−10 pts · Medium
DP-CL
🌀
Confusing Language

Double negatives like "uncheck to opt-out" or "untick if you do not want" make it impossible to understand what you're agreeing to.

−10 pts · Medium
DP-NG
🔔
Nagging

The consent banner reappears on repeat visits even after a user has already made their choice — wearing users down.

−10 pts · Medium

Consent Asymmetry Index

A quantitative score measuring how many times harder it is to reject consent vs. accept it.

CAI = (clicks_reject − 1) / 1

CAI 0.00 = perfect parity  |  CAI 1.00 = 2× more effort to reject

LEGAL COVERAGE

Every detected dark pattern is mapped to the specific article or section of the relevant regulation — giving you citation-ready evidence.

🇪🇺
GDPR
General Data Protection Regulation · EU 2016/679

The world's most comprehensive data protection law, in force since May 2018. Applies to any organisation processing EU residents' personal data, regardless of where the organisation is located.

Art. 4(11) Defines valid consent: freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous indication of agreement.
Art. 7(3) Withdrawal of consent must be as easy as giving it. Detected by CAI score and DP-RM / DP-FA.
Art. 13 & 14 Transparency obligations. Hidden information (DP-HI) violates this directly.
Recital 32 Pre-ticked boxes (DP-PT) do not constitute valid consent under GDPR.
Art. 6(1)(a) Lawful basis for processing. Pre-consent cookie firing (DP-PC) is a direct violation.
🇮🇳
DPDP
Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 · India

India's landmark data protection legislation passed in August 2023. Applies to processing of digital personal data within India and to processing outside India if related to offering goods/services in India.

Section 5 Notice requirement. The data fiduciary must give clear, standalone notice — detected via banner presence and DP-HI.
Section 6 Consent must be free, specific, informed and unambiguous. Covers DP-FA, DP-PT, DP-VM, DP-CL, DP-NG, DP-FH.
Section 7 Legitimate uses of data. Information about these must not be hidden (DP-HI) from the data principal.
Section 9 Rights of data principal — right to withdraw consent easily. Maps to DP-FA and DP-RM detectors.
