# Privacy Policy for ConsentFair

**Effective Date:** February 2026  
**Developer:** Nikita Tapali | Amity University 

## 1. Overview
ConsentFair is an open-source, academic research tool designed to audit website cookie consent banners. We are fundamentally committed to user privacy. **ConsentFair does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.**

## 2. Data Processing
All heuristic scanning, visual asymmetry detection, and score calculations are performed **100% locally** on your device within your browser. No data ever leaves your computer. We do not use external servers, analytics trackers, or third-party APIs.

## 3. Extension Permissions Justification
To function autonomously, ConsentFair requires the following minimal browser permissions:
* `activeTab` & `<all_urls>`: Required strictly to read the Document Object Model (DOM) of the current page you are visiting to locate cookie banners and capture a localized viewport screenshot for the forensic report.
* `downloads`: Required strictly to allow you to save the generated HTML audit report directly to your local hard drive. 

## 4. Contact
Because we do not collect data, we have no user records to delete or manage. If you have questions about this privacy policy or the open-source code, please open an issue on our [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/NIKITATAPALI/ConsentFair).
